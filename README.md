# AM Home Care and Nursing Services

> **"Compassionate Care for Your Loved Ones"**

A clean, modern, and responsive website demo for **AM Home Care and Nursing Services**, based in Chennai.

---

## 🩺 Overview

This demo website provides a professional online presence for AM Home Care and Nursing Services while full digital services are being finalized.

- **Status**: Website Under Process Demo
- **Location**: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
- **Tech Stack**: HTML5, CSS3 (Vanilla), Vanilla JavaScript

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Exact Brand Identity**: Incorporates the official AM Home Care and Nursing Services logo and emblem.
- **Healthcare Color Palette**: Deep royal blue, medical green, and clean white.
- **Key Sections**:
  - **Header**: Official logo and quick navigation (Home, Services, Contact) with a mobile drawer menu.
  - **Hero**: Clean presentation with Chennai location indicator, call-to-action buttons (*Call Now*, *WhatsApp*), and a home care visual.
  - **Services**: 4 core care services:
    - Home Nursing
    - Elderly / Senior Care
    - Patient Care
    - Post-Hospitalisation Care
  - **About**: "Professional Care. Personal Attention."
  - **Website Under Process**: Highlighted status banner with an animated loading progress indicator.
  - **Contact CTA**: Direct phone and WhatsApp communication channels.
  - **Footer**: Brand statement and copyright.
